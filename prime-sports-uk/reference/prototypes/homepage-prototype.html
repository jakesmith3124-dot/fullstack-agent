Parent FAQ

Where will camps run?

We're finalising 3 to 5 venues across West Yorkshire. Sign up for early access and tell us your area so we can plan around demand.

Can my child come if they're not sporty?

Yes. Camps are built for all abilities. Plenty of children come to try new things and make friends, not to win.

Can I book single days?

Yes. You can book individual days or the whole week, and add siblings to the same booking.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, on camps booked far enough ahead. All instalments finish before the camp starts.

My child has medical or additional needs. Can they attend?

Tell us about any medical conditions, allergies or SEND when you book, and we'll talk to you before the camp about the support they need.

Can I use Tax-Free Childcare or vouchers?

We're setting this up and will confirm before bookings open.

What it's about

Better players, more confident kids

G.O.A.T is for children who love football and want to improve. Every player gets coaching pitched at their level, with clear goals and regular feedback on how they're progressing.

01

Individual progression

Each player works on their own targets, so beginners and confident players both improve.

02

Ball mastery

Lots of touches: dribbling, first touch, passing and finishing, built up session by session.

03

Game understanding

Decision-making, positioning and teamwork in small-sided games.

What to expect

How the academy will work

Weekly sessions

Regular coached sessions in small groups, organised by age and ability.

Days, times and venue to confirm

Monthly membership

A simple monthly membership you can cancel at any time, with options for one or two sessions a week.

Prices to confirm

Free trial session

Every new player can try a session before joining, so you can see if it's the right fit.

G.O.A.T is about enjoyment, confidence and steady improvement. We don't promise professional contracts or academy places, but we will help every player get better.

Booking & payments

How do I book?

Choose a camp in the Holiday Camps section, pick your days and add your children. You'll get a confirmation email straight away.

Can I book single days?

Yes. Book one day, a few days or the full week.

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes, a discount is applied automatically when you book more than one child.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, on camps booked far enough in advance. All payments finish before the camp starts.

What if I need to cancel?

Our cancellation and refund policy explains how much notice you need to give and what you'll get back.

On the day

What should my child bring?

A packed lunch and snacks (nut-free, please), a refillable water bottle, trainers, comfortable sports clothes, a waterproof or sun cream depending on the weather, and any medication clearly labelled with their name.

What time is drop-off and collection?

Times are shown on each camp's booking page. Please arrive on time, as sessions start promptly.

Who can collect my child?

Only adults you've named on the booking. If someone else needs to collect, let us know in advance. We won't release a child to anyone not on the list.

What happens if I'm late collecting?

A member of staff will always stay with your child. Please call us as soon as you know you'll be late.

Late collection policy to confirm
What if it rains?

We have indoor space at our venues and plan wet-weather activities, so camps go ahead whatever the weather.

Confirm indoor space at each venue

Health & safety

Are your coaches DBS-checked and qualified?

Yes. Every coach working with children has an enhanced DBS check and relevant coaching qualifications.

Is there a first aider on site?

Yes, there's a qualified first aider at every session.

My child has a medical condition or allergy. Can they attend?

Yes. Tell us when you book, and we'll make sure staff know and that any medication is kept safe and to hand. For more complex needs we'll speak to you before the camp.

Do you support children with SEND?

We aim to include every child. Tell us about your child's needs when you book and we'll talk to you about the right support.

Will photos of my child be shared?

Only if you give permission when you book. You can change your mind at any time.

Everything else

What ages do you cater for?

Our holiday camps are for ages 4 to 11, and Prime Camps (from February 2027) will run for ages 5 to 17 with separate groups by age.

My child isn't very sporty. Will they enjoy it?

Yes. Our sessions are built for all abilities, and lots of children come to try new things and make friends.

Do you work with schools?

Yes. We offer PE support, breakfast, lunchtime and after-school clubs and enrichment. Get in touch to talk about your school.
